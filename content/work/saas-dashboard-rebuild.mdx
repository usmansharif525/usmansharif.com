## The challenge

Acme Analytics (placeholder client) had grown past their original internal dashboard. Pages took seconds to load, workflows required too many clicks, and the engineering team was stuck firefighting instead of shipping features.

## Approach

I partnered with their product lead to map the highest-friction journeys, then rebuilt the dashboard as a modern Next.js app with:

- Server-rendered list views for fast first paint
- Clear information hierarchy for daily operators
- Typed API layer against their existing backend
- Incremental rollout so teams weren't blocked during migration

## Outcome

Operators got a faster, clearer tool. Engineering got a maintainable front-end foundation. Metrics above are illustrative placeholders — swap in your real numbers when you publish this case study.

## Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, REST + webhooks, Vercel.
