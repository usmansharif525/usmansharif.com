## The challenge

Northline Tools (placeholder) had a solid product and almost no organic presence. Blog posts were sporadic, technical SEO basics were missing, and nobody owned a measurement loop.

## Approach

Working as a growth partner with an engineering lens, I:

- Fixed crawlability, sitemap, metadata, and Core Web Vitals issues
- Designed a topic cluster around high-intent buyer questions
- Set up a lightweight MDX publishing pipeline so content shipped weekly
- Instrumenting search Console + analytics events tied to signup steps

Organic traffic and qualified signups grew as the content library compounded. Numbers above are placeholders — replace them with your real campaign results.
