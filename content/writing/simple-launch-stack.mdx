You don't need a perfect stack to launch. You need something fast, measurable, and easy to change.

## Baseline I often recommend

1. **Next.js (or Astro)** for the marketing site or product shell
2. **Clear analytics** with a handful of meaningful events — not fifty vanity ones
3. **One primary CTA** and a way to book or buy without friction
4. **SEO basics** from day one: metadata, sitemap, performance, sensible URLs
5. **A weekly feedback loop** — what shipped, what moved, what to try next

## The trap

Overbuilding the product before validating demand. Or pouring budget into ads before the page and tracking can tell you what's working.

## The point

Ship the smallest honest version. Instrument it. Improve it. Whether the work is "software" or "growth," the rhythm is the same.
