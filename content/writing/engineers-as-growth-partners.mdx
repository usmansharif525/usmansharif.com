Most growth problems are systems problems in disguise.

Tracking is broken. Landing pages can't ship without a two-week queue. SEO recommendations ignore how the CMS actually works. Experiments die because nobody can implement the variant.

That's the gap I keep seeing — and it's why I'm exploring digital growth work without stepping away from software.

## What engineers bring to growth

- **Measurement you can trust.** Event schemas, attribution, and pipelines that don't quietly lie.
- **Ship speed.** Pages, experiments, and automations that don't wait on a ticket backlog.
- **Honest constraints.** Knowing when a "marketing fix" is really a product or architecture problem.

## What this means for clients

You can hire me for software. You can hire me for growth. Or you can hire me for both — one partner who understands the product and the pipeline.
